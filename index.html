Claude

I started as an architect. Then a sculptor. An MFA in Design at UC Davis — spatial work, light, exhibition environments. The same question followed me through every material: how does a space make you feel something without explaining itself?

The materials changed. Stone. Bronze. Light. Code. AI. The instinct didn’t — make what surrounds people easier to read, easier to move through, easier to trust. The tools evolve. I evolve with them.
