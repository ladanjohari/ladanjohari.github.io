The idea

Supervision is something you do between other things, so it had to be somewhere you already look, not an app you remember to open. The menu bar is for attention.

The window is for structure: every goal, the tasks it broke into, what each one waits on, and the checks that close it.

Why it matters

  • When agents work on their own, it is hard to tell what they did and what is waiting on you.
  • People who run several at once have to stand behind what the agents produced.

Try it

It runs on macOS 14 or newer. Two steps, and the second one is a double click. 

git clone https://github.com/ladanjohari/substrate.git
cd substrate && open "Open Substrate.command"

The build is not signed yet, so the first open asks whether you meant it. Right click the file, choose Open, then Open again.
