Session Indicator hover — two cohesion directions

Right now SI’s hover is the photographic menu-bar capture while the other three are flat designed prototypes. Two ways to make the set read as one family. Both keep SI’s story: the pill lives in the menu bar, click it, the sessions popover springs out.

Direction A · SI joins the flat family new prototype

SI redrawn in the same language as the other three: light surface, real anatomy, one cursor interaction. Cursor clicks the pill, the popover springs from it (origin at the pill, per the HIG), four sessions cascade in with their live states. In dark mode it would get a dark variant like the others.

Direction B · the family goes dark, like SI already rendered

Keep SI’s current capture, and lean the whole hover set dark: the dark variants built for dark mode become the only clips, in both themes. The set reads as one moody family and matches the dark static thumbnails; the trade-off is losing the light “real product” look you approved for PF and Xcode.

My recommendation

Direction A. The light prototypes are the distinctive thing on the page: they read instantly, they match the “real product” fidelity you pushed for, and dark mode is already handled by the theme-swap. Rebuilding SI flat is one prototype; going dark-everywhere throws away the light set you just iterated five rounds to get. A also keeps the nice reveal: dark illustration → bright living product.
