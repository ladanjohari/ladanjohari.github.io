Selected Work · visual directions

Two complete systems for the thumbnails, each shown across all four projects in the live row. The question underneath: does the visual say what the project does at a glance?

Direction A — Designed motif (recommended)

Each thumbnail is a purpose-built miniature of the project’s one core mechanic, drawn in a shared visual grammar: same dark canvas, same 12px radius, disciplined accent, hairline depth. They read as a family and stay legible at this size.

Apple/HIG: one idea per image, deference, consistent system. Real UI lives in the hover clip and the case study, where there’s room to breathe.
