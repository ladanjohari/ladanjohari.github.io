My art journey

I began my career as an architect, shaping spaces with precision and intent. But after earning my master’s degree, I felt an undeniable pull toward creating my own art. That turning point led me to Parviz Tanavoli, a pioneer of Iranian contemporary art, whose mentorship profoundly shaped my vision.

Tanavoli’s modern reinterpretation of Iranian heritage resonated deeply with me, inspiring a fusion of influences: his approach, my architectural foundation, and my own exploration of form. That blend lets me make sculptures and objects that carry cultural narratives while embracing bold, structural expression.

Sculptures

I work primarily in bronze and ceramic, combining the permanence of metal with the tactile warmth of clay. My forms draw from architectural geometry, cultural heritage, and personal narratives, balancing bold structure with subtle emotion.
